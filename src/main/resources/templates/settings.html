Settings & Candidate Profile

Manage your CV data, AI profile extraction, and Swiss job search matching criteria.

💡 How It Works
1. Provide Your Information

Upload your CV in PDF format, or type your skills, background, and career preferences directly into the text box (or both).

2. AI Profile Extraction

Click "✨ Generate Profile". Gemini AI extracts technical skills, seniority, languages, and suggests a matching Swiss job search query.

3. Iterative Refinement

Add or refine your preferences anytime. When regenerating, your existing profile is intelligently updated and enriched, not erased!
